The idea in 20 seconds

Everyone taps a PIN, or types a phrase, in their own rhythm: how long the finger stays down, the gap between taps, where it lands. Innate runs silently under the wallet's PIN and confirm screens, learns the owner's pattern, and checks it on every transfer. A bot, or someone who only knows the PIN, does not match, so Innate asks for more or holds the transfer. Model: LightGBM, trained on real people and simulated bots.
